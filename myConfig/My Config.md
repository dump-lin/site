## Filters

There are only two filter plugins:

- **ExplicitPublish** - in use
	- only publishes pages that are tagged `publish: true` in the Frontmatter
	- no config options
- **RemoveDrafts** - not in use
	- filters out all pages tagged `draft: true` in the Frontmatter
	- no config options
